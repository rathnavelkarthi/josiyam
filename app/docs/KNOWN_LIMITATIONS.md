# Known Limitations: Jyotisha

**Date:** October 10, 2026

1. **Backend Connectivity:** In offline environments or without a running FastAPI container, the astronomical calculation engine falls back to local on-device mathematical calculations.
2. **Biometric Inference:** CameraX palm and face scanning process images on-device via MediaPipe. Image quality scores depend on lighting and device camera resolution.
3. **Astrological Interpretations:** Traditional readings and source citations are provided for educational and exploratory purposes and should not be interpreted as absolute deterministic predictions or medical advice.
